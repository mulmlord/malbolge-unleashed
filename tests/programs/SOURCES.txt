Test programs for `malm run` (unleashed mode, plain text loader format).

halt2.mal
    Hand-written 2-cell program "jv" (MovD, Hlt); loader text "(P". Public domain / own work.

cat5.mal
    Own work. A straight-line cat of exactly 5 bytes: (In Out) x5, then Hlt, written in normalized
    form "/</</</</<v" and converted to loader text with the spec's xlat1 rule. It halts, so it is
    the binary-safe pipe test (it is NOT a general cat; a cat that halts on EOF needs a loop with a
    conditional and is far more complex).
    cat5_input.bin / cat5_expected.bin: the bytes 'a' 'b' 'c' 0x00 0xFF.

cat_nostop.mal
    The classic Malbolge cat that does not stop on EOF (in unleashed mode it copies stdin and then
    writes byte 0xA8 forever: A = ...222 and Out writes A mod 256 = 168).
    Source: https://esolangs.org/wiki/Malbolge ("Cat" section). Esolangs wiki content is CC0.

hello.mal
    "Hello, world." in classic Malbolge; it halts in unleashed mode after 45 steps with n = 10.
    Source: https://esolangs.org/wiki/Hello_world_program_in_esoteric_languages_(H-M)
    (Malbolge section). Esolangs wiki content is CC0.

beer99_width.mal
    Hisashi Iizawa et al.'s 99 bottles of beer in classic Malbolge, byte-identical to the
    <pre> block of the Malbolge section on https://esolangs.org/wiki/99_bottles_of_beer
    (esolangs wiki content is CC0). In unleashed mode it ratchets n up and fails with E_WIDTH at
    step 4258 with n = 39 and no output (spec 2.2, non-normative note); used as the natural
    E_WIDTH test program.
